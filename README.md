# How Long Are You Having Lived

A simple, interactive web tool that calculates exactly how long you have been alive down to the second. Enter your birth date and time to discover your age in years, months, days, hours, minutes, and seconds.

## 🚀 Live Demo

Check out the live demo: [https://www.xn--msiu-goa8b.vn/github/how-long-are-you-having-lived](https://www.xn--msiu-goa8b.vn/github/how-long-are-you-having-lived)

## ✨ Features

- **Birth Date Input** – Enter your date of birth using a date picker
- **Optional Birth Time** – Specify your exact time of birth for more precise calculations
- **Comprehensive Results** – See your age displayed in years, months, days, hours, minutes, and seconds
- **Real-Time Calculation** – Results update instantly when you click the calculate button
- **Clean Interface** – Simple, user-friendly design with a clear layout
- **Responsive** – Works on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices

## 📝 How It Works

1. **Enter your birth date** – Use the date picker to select your date of birth (required)
2. **Enter your birth time** – Optionally enter the exact time of your birth using the time picker
3. **Click "Tính" (Calculate)** – The tool computes the total time you have been alive
4. **View the results** – Your age is displayed in multiple units:
   - Years
   - Months
   - Days
   - Hours
   - Minutes
   - Seconds

The calculation is based on the current date and time, providing an accurate measurement of your lifespan down to the second.
